# Code of Conduct

All mycelium-ha repositories follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers of the affected repository, through a GitHub private vulnerability report marked "Code of Conduct" or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, lock discussions, or block participants who violate this code.
